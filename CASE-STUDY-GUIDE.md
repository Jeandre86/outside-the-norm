# Ontwrp case studies

## Writing structure

Lead with a project-specific idea, then establish the client, scope, and challenge. Explain the design decisions and show the work alongside the explanation. End with deliverables and verified outcomes. Brand identity projects do not need to pretend that user research or usability testing happened. Empty chapters stay hidden.

World Greatest Game currently contains explicitly labelled illustrative copy and preview imagery. Replace the invented brief and narrative with approved facts and client artwork before removing the placeholder label. No client endorsement or performance metric has been invented.

## Layouts

- **Editorial:** split introduction and project facts, clear narrative chapters, interleaved imagery.
- **Visual Showcase:** artwork first, centred introduction, large single-column chapters, paired gallery.
- **UX Journey:** numbered chapters with a chapter index, research and decisions shown in sequence.
- **Brand Transformation:** dark opening, focused narrative, and an outcome section with stronger emphasis.

In Studio, open a project's Case study section. Choose a layout, reorder chapters with Up/Down, choose an image arrangement, and save the project. To reuse a configuration, give it a name and select **Save as reusable layout**. Saved layouts store presentation settings only, never another client's copy or images. Apply them from the saved-layout selector; reload saved layouts if another editor has added one. Up to 30 named presets are supported.

## References and their contribution

These are structural influences, not copied text, designs, claims, or client assets.

- [Arkive — CTRLR/Refresh](https://www.ctrlr.studio/work/arkive): clear scope followed by problem, approach, work, and outcome.
- [CHANGECARS — Elemental](https://www.elemental.agency/our-work/change-cars-bespoke-platform-custom-web-development): specific solution chapters supported by project visuals.
- [SnapScan — Now Boarding](https://www.nowboarding.io/showcase/snapscan-website): explain user journeys and interface decisions before presenting final screens.
- [Fivewest — Funtown](https://www.funtownstudio.com/portfolio/fivewest): discovery, design direction, product consistency, and clarity about the studio's actual scope.
- [LeadBot — Mandelo](https://www.mandelo.agency/werk/leadbot): connect identity decisions to their application across a product and its communications.
- [MiWay Blink — Isoflow](https://www.isoflow.co.za/our-work/miway-blink): separate services and outputs; show how a system works across different touchpoints.
- [Sustainable Seas Trust — Superseed](https://superseedstudio.com/project/sustainable-seas-trust/): concise project framing with an explicit takeaway/results section. The text extraction showed counter values of zero; those are not treated as verified results.
- [Descript — Ramotion](https://www.ramotion.com/descript-brand-identity-and-web-design/): explain the reasoning behind a mark and how the design system supports ongoing use.
- [House of Biscuits — Magnetic](https://magneticcreative.com/rebranding-house-of-biscuits): present creative directions, refinement, and application as stages in a transformation story.
- [Oushk Pharmacy — mtc](https://www.mtc.co.uk/our-work/oushk-pharmacy/): distinguish challenge, solution, measurable impact, and lessons learned. Its published metrics belong to that project and are not reused for Ontwrp.

## Motion

Short, one-time entrance animations for selected content, a subtle hero fade, and hover feedback on cards/buttons. Native scrolling is preserved. Reduced-motion preferences disable these effects. The editor is excluded from entrance animations.
